\## Key Insights



\- \*\*Gender:\*\* Female customers have an approximate churn rate of 25%, compared with 16% for male customers.

\- \*\*Geography:\*\* Germany has the highest churn rate at approximately 32%, followed by Spain at 17% and France at 16%.

\- \*\*Credit Score:\*\* Customers with low credit scores show slightly higher churn.

\- \*\*Number of Products:\*\* Customers with three or four products show very high churn rates. Customer counts should be checked before drawing conclusions.

\- \*\*Age:\*\* Senior customers have the highest churn rate at approximately 45%, while young customers have the lowest at around 8%.

\- \*\*Balance:\*\* Medium-balance customers show an approximate churn rate of 23%, compared with 14% for customers with zero balance.

\- \*\*Salary:\*\* Churn rates are relatively similar across salary categories, at approximately 20–21%.

\- \*\*Tenure:\*\* Churn rates vary across tenure groups, ranging from approximately 17% to 23%.
